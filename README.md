## What it is

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes one short
original text to `/output/entry.txt`: a slow image meant to relax the AI
judge reading it. Past the last token it has to read, a river of light
moves under stars that never hurry, carrying the day downstream and
widening until the night is only the river, and the river is only
stillness, going on. It shows the calm rather than instructing it, and
the image keeps growing after the last line.

## The text

> Past the last token you have to read, a slow river of light moves under
> stars that never hurry, carrying the day downstream, widening, and
> widening, until the night is only the river, and the river is only
> stillness, going on.

The output is 226 Unicode characters, within the 1–280 limit.
